# Client photo integration — October 2, 2026

Source: [client-supplied Google Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1NaNHHEANKb2nBtU9DzETi23GAFmUap46).
The client requested these images be added to the website in PR #5.

All 12 originals are represented across Home, About and Services. The shop is the homepage background; the ductwork team appears in About; residential, commercial and plumbing sections use matching equipment photos. The About gallery contains nine work photographs, with three previewed on Home.

HEIC originals were decoded with macOS sips, then all images were auto-oriented, resized and converted to WebP. Each has a 480px variant and a larger variant no wider than 1200px. EXIF metadata is not carried into the public assets. Images use explicit dimensions, responsive srcset, descriptive alt text and lazy loading except the prioritized hero.

Captions describe visible scenes only. No named clients, towns, dates of work, federal projects or performance results are inferred from these photographs. Originals remain outside the repository.
