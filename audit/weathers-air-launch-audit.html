Launch decision

Do not launch the currently deployed preview unchanged.

The visual foundation is strong, but its contact form silently discards requests. The GitHub version is substantially newer and fixes several preview defects, yet still needs form configuration, production-domain changes, migration redirects, and a final production smoke test.

Business goal: qualified calls and booked service jobs in Columbus, Mississippi. Prioritize reliable lead capture and preservation of existing search visibility before expanding content.

Audit date: September 30, 2026. Existing domain: weathersairconditioning.com. Preview: weathers.aurexagency.com. Intended primary domain: https://weathersair.com.

What was tested

Reviewed all six marketing routes in a rendered browser: Home, Services, Reviews, About, Contact, and Shop. Inspected deployed JavaScript, rendered metadata, schema, links, contact controls, mobile navigation at 390 × 844, mobile call-bar placement, service anchors, HTTP status codes, robots, sitemap, and domain readiness. Built and rendered the provided repository at commit b7b779d1669639183c9686110ea49c19882697fc. Ran lint, TypeScript, all existing tests, production build, and dependency audit.

Passed: repository lint/typecheck/build; 18 tests across four test files. All six preview routes return HTTP 200. Phone links use the supplied business number. Service anchors eventually land correctly; the mobile menu opens and expands. Shop catalog eventually renders 494 items. No broken images were observed on Services, Reviews, or About in the rendered checks.

Limits: no Search Console, GA4, Google Business Profile admin, hosting configuration, inbox, CRM, or DNS account inspection. No real service requests, subscriptions, phone calls, or orders were sent. Checkout/payment completion is unverified. PageSpeed API returned 429 quota exhausted, so no Lighthouse score or field Core Web Vitals pass is claimed. Old-site homepage and navigation were retrieved, but later old-site crawl requests encountered CleanTalk anti-crawler protection/403; the migration inventory below must be reconciled with CMS, analytics, and Search Console exports. No comprehensive backlink, local rank-grid, or multi-platform AI visibility baseline was available.

Keep the existing navy, blue and amber palette, readable typography and persistent call/schedule pattern. Improve the ordering and authenticity of evidence rather than redesigning everything immediately.

  2. Keep two choices: Call Now and Request Service. Do not imply confirmed booking when the form only requests a callback. Keep emergency copy phone-first, consistently.
  3. Show a verified review rating and business experience close to the main CTA. Link proof to its real source. Use authentic staff, trucks, work sites and award photos; do not present an illustration as documentary evidence.
  4. Move procurement detail below the main homeowner journey or to a dedicated capability page. Recommended homepage order: service/location promise → proof → common service needs → how scheduling works → relevant reviews → maintenance → service area → concise about/FAQ → contact.

  7. Recheck keyboard focus, labels, error announcements, contrast, 200% zoom, 320/390/430/768px layouts and mobile sticky overlays. The repository improves accessibility, but it is not a complete accessibility certification.

Local Google search plan

The website supports local discovery, but Google Business Profile accuracy and reputation need equal attention. Google describes local ranking in terms of relevance, distance and prominence; no website change can guarantee the top map position everywhere. Google local ranking guidance.

  • Retain the established Business Profile and reviews; update its website to the new domain after launch. Verify name, phone, address, category, office/emergency hours, service list and holiday hours.
  • Choose the most accurate primary category for the core business; add plumbing/electrical categories only if truly provided. Do not add keywords to the business name or fabricate satellite offices.
  • Confirm Facebook/Yelp/Google profile ownership and align business name, phone, address and URL across them, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, legitimate local chambers and manufacturer directories.
  • Ask customers consistently for honest Google reviews after completed jobs, without incentives or filtering by satisfaction. Respond helpfully. Keep website testimonials faithful to their source.
  • Publish real project photos and short case studies: problem, area served, diagnosis, work completed and result. Obtain permission and avoid exposing private home details.
  • Earn relevant links from legitimate partner, supplier, chamber and community relationships. Confirm Amana/TVA/award affiliations before seeking or displaying endorsements.

AI search visibility plan

Use the same accurate, useful public pages for people and search systems. Google says its AI search features build on foundational SEO and do not require special AI files or markup. Google AI search guidance.

  1. Make the business unambiguous: consistent name, location, contact details, verified service area, ownership/history and credentials. Use stable production-domain entity IDs in LocalBusiness schema.
  2. Make content accessible: prerender marketing pages; allow Googlebot/Bingbot and relevant search crawlers through robots and the CDN/WAF. Check logs rather than assuming Allow rules defeat bot challenges.
  3. Separate search from training: OAI-SearchBot governs ChatGPT search crawling; GPTBot is a separate training preference. Training access is not required for search inclusion. OpenAI crawler documentation.
  4. Answer actual customer questions: service coverage, brands serviced, after-hours process, maintenance inclusions, repair versus replacement factors, diagnostic fees and appointment expectations. Use natural headings, clear paragraphs, useful comparison tables and named technical reviewers.
  5. Publish firsthand evidence: two real Columbus-area project writeups monthly if capacity allows, with technician input and authentic photos. Add factual revisions when information changes, not cosmetic freshness dates.
  6. Support claims: replace generic health promises in duct-cleaning copy with careful inspection criteria. EPA says routine duct cleaning is not proven to prevent health problems and evidence for efficiency benefits from cleaning ducts alone is limited. EPA guidance.
  7. Maintain structured data: accurate HVACBusiness with relevant additional types, address/hours/sameAs and service information; breadcrumbs matching site structure. FAQs help visitors, but do not promise FAQ rich results. Google’s current update log says the FAQ rich-result feature was removed. Google documentation updates.
  8. Measure mentions and citations separately: monthly fixed queries across Google AI features, ChatGPT search and Perplexity; record date, location/context, exact prompt, cited URLs, competitor mentions and factual accuracy. This audit did not establish a current AI share-of-voice baseline.

Initial monitoring prompts: “Who repairs AC in Columbus Mississippi?”, “HVAC maintenance Columbus MS”, “Does Weathers Air Conditioning offer emergency service?”, “Who installs HVAC for businesses in Columbus MS?”, “Duct cleaning versus duct sealing in Columbus MS”, and “Weathers Air Conditioning phone and service area.” Do not prioritize llms.txt, artificial keyword repetition or mass-produced town pages over these fundamentals.

Measurement and performance

Primary outcomes: qualified calls, valid form requests, booked jobs and revenue by acquisition source/service. Phone-link clicks are an intent signal, not completed calls. Record the office outcome of each lead.

Events: page_view once per SPA navigation; click_to_call with page/placement; form_start; generate_lead only after successful server acceptance; form_error; directions_click; shop outbound/product/cart events where vendor supports them. Never send names, email addresses, phone numbers or freeform messages to analytics. Use UTMs on the GBP website link and maintain the stable public business number.

Weekly: compare old and new Search Console properties during migration, indexed canonical URLs, query/page impressions and clicks, top landing-page performance, 404/redirect errors and qualified leads. Separate brand from nonbrand queries and account for seasonality.

Performance evidence: built repository main application JS is ~38 KB gzip, React chunk ~53 KB and motion chunk ~42 KB, excluding third-party code. Hero WebP ~155 KB; largest listed service image ~265 KB. These are bundle measurements, not page speed scores. Existing code uses lazy-loaded routes, WebP images and a high-priority hero image, which are useful foundations.

Next performance checks: run PageSpeed/Lighthouse on final Home, Services, Contact and Shop on mobile and desktop; inspect real-user data when available. Aim for LCP ≤2.5 seconds, INP ≤200 ms and CLS ≤0.1 at the 75th percentile. Focus on above-fold image/font delivery, third-party commerce script cost, motion/reveal behavior and responsive images. Keep content visible if animation fails; verify counters do not expose misleading zero values to assistive technology. No field-performance pass was verified here.
